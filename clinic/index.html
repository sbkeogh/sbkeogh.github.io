Before you begin

You are the clinic student. The client has come in to talk about an advance health care directive. You know only what is on the intake card. Interview as you would in the office: introduce yourself, build rapport, ask open questions, one at a time, and listen.

How it works. Hold the round button (or the space bar) while you speak, then let go. The client answers out loud. You can type instead at any time. When you are done, press End interview to get your transcript.
